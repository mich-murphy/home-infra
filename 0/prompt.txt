### CF mapping (both apps, ~30 CFs each)

Every stock CF matched a TRaSH `trash_id` by name; where specs differed it was only guide content drift (e.g. missing newer release groups in Remux/WEB tiers — Recyclarr will update these to current guide content, which is expected/desired). Two real discoveries versus the task's assumptions:
- **"Wrong Language" is no longer bespoke** — TRaSH Guides now ships an official CF with byte-identical specs. Only **"Dual Audio (Original + English)"** and **"Dual Audio (Release Title)"** remain genuinely bespoke (both apps), supplied locally.
- **Sonarr's "Repack v2"/"Repack v3"** matched the *current* guide's `Repack2`/`Repack3` by regex intent only — not by name, and not by exact spec (guide content evolved). Recyclarr matches existing-vs-guide CFs by name, so referencing those trash_ids would have made Recyclarr **create duplicate CFs** rather than update in place (verified live: preview showed `Repack2 (0): 0 -&gt; 6 (New)`). Fixed by supplying them locally under their live name/spec instead.
